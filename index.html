Privacy
Why DenseZK

Social networks sell
your data. We don't.

Every app you use today asks you to hand over personal information just to participate. DenseZK flips that model. Your phone does the verification locally, shares only what's necessary, and keeps everything else locked away — permanently.

01
Your phone is the vault

All verification happens on your device. Nothing is sent to a server to be processed, stored, or sold. The computation stays local — only a small, unreadable proof is ever transmitted.

02
You own your identity

Your credentials — who you are, what groups you belong to, what you've done — live on your device. DenseZK uses them to verify a claim, then discards the underlying data immediately.

03
No backdoors, no ceremonies

DenseZK is built on a proof system that requires zero trust from any party to set up. There's no committee that could be corrupted, no hidden key that could be stolen.

04
Built for real social apps

Reputation, group membership, consent, age — the things social networks currently store about you — can all be verified privately, without ever creating a data profile.

Verify
How it works

Your device checks.
Nobody else does.

Think of DenseZK as a notary that lives inside your phone. It checks your credentials, produces a signed statement that they're valid, and hands over only that statement — never the credentials themselves.

01 —
Check

Your phone runs the logic.

Whatever needs to be verified — your age, your membership, your reputation — is checked entirely on your device using a tiny, auditable program. Nothing leaves at this stage.

02 —
Seal

A proof is generated.

Your device produces a compact cryptographic proof — a mathematically guaranteed statement that the check passed. It contains no personal information. It cannot be reverse-engineered.

03 —
Share

Only the proof travels.

The proof is sent. The data that produced it never is. Anyone can verify the proof is valid without learning anything about you — and the original data is discarded from memory.

Build
Use cases

What becomes
possible.

Any moment a social app currently asks you to trust it with personal data, DenseZK offers an alternative — prove what's needed, share nothing else.

01
Verify your age privately

Prove you're over 18 without uploading an ID or birthday to any server. The app learns only that the check passed — nothing more.

02
Match without being watched

Two people can prove mutual interest before either profile is revealed. No platform sees who showed interest in whom — consent comes first, visibility follows.

03
Build reputation without exposure

Prove your trust score or activity history meets a threshold without revealing the underlying actions. Credibility without surveillance.

04
Join groups anonymously

Prove you're a member of a community — a university, a neighbourhood, a club — without disclosing which member you are or what else you belong to.

05
Vote and react without a trace

Likes, votes, and reactions are provably unique — no one can cheat the count — but they're not tied to your identity or stored in any central database.

06
Connect without a data broker

Prove shared connections or proximity in a social graph without the graph itself ever being exposed. Relationships verified, identities protected.

Design
Our principles

Small enough
to trust completely.

Deliberately minimal.

DenseZK does a small number of things and does them provably. A smaller system has fewer places to hide bugs, fewer ways to be compromised, and a smaller surface for anything to go wrong.

No trust required.

The proof system underpinning DenseZK was chosen specifically because it needs no trusted setup — no founding committee, no secret parameters. It works because the math works, full stop.

Runs on a mid-range phone.

Privacy technology that only works on high-end hardware isn't real privacy. DenseZK is engineered to be fast and small enough to run on a 3-year-old Android — because everyone deserves it, not just premium users.

Open to inspection.

Every component is open source. Researchers, security engineers, and curious builders can read every line. Privacy claims that can't be checked are just promises — we'd rather be audited.

The future of social
is private by default.

DenseZK is open source and in active development. The first public release ships with everything builders need — full documentation, example apps, and a React Native SDK ready to drop into any mobile project.
